Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. What the app does

Spirit Level is a precision utility app providing a bubble level, camera alignment view, and an on-screen smart ruler to measure angles, slopes, and lengths directly on your device.

2. Data we collect

This app has no user accounts and does not collect or upload your personal data. Tilt sensor readings and camera images are processed only on your device in real time, and nothing is stored on external servers or sent to us.

3. Camera and sensor permissions

Camera permission is used only while the Camera Level feature is open to display the live alignment overlay. Motion and orientation sensors are used only while leveling features are open, and your settings are saved only locally on your device.

4. Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device advertising ID to display and measure ads. Users in the EEA and UK can review and manage their ad consent choices at any time from Settings.

5. Purchases

The one-time Remove Ads - Lifetime purchase is handled entirely by Google Play on your Google account. We do not collect, receive, or see your payment card or billing details.

6. Children

Spirit Level is a general utility tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy in future versions of the app. Whenever changes are made, the updated date will be shown at the top of this page.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach us via ARSpiritLevel@gmail.com.
